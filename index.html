About

I'm an instructional designer in the Seattle–Tacoma area with more than eight years of experience building online learning for colleges and for a global logistics company. My work sits where three things meet: solid instructional design, hands-on production, and a real understanding of what it's like to be a learner who is new to the material.

That last part comes from teaching. I teach Technology Literacy and ESL to adult learners at Clover Park Technical College, guiding first-time computer users through Word, Zoom, browsers, and online learning platforms. It's a daily lab in inclusive design, and the habits carry into everything else I build.

On the design side, I've administered Canvas at two Washington colleges, built a 25-module instructor onboarding course from the ground up, co-developed an AI literacy certificate that earned a college innovation award, and designed corporate eLearning that reached 20,000 employees. I manage projects through the full ADDIE cycle, usually several at once, and produce my own video and graphic assets.

The part I enjoy most is partnering with faculty and subject matter experts: taking what they know and shaping it into something learners can actually use.

How I work

  • Start with the learner and the outcome, then design backward to the activities and content.
  • Run the full ADDIE cycle with stakeholder checkpoints at storyboard, prototype, and pilot.
  • Build accessibility in from the first draft: alt text, captions, contrast, keyboard navigation, readable structure.
  • Treat faculty and SMEs as co-designers, not content sources.
  • Use AI tools where they help learners, and be plain about where they don't.
  • Manage scope, timeline, and quality with PMP discipline, without letting process crowd out judgment.
